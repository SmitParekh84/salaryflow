# SalaryFlow Brand Brief

## Brief purpose

This document defines what SalaryFlow solves, who it serves, how the brand should
feel, and what success looks like. Use it as the strategic foundation for the
logo, app icon, wordmark, color system, typography, and wider visual identity.

## Brand in one sentence

SalaryFlow turns a monthly salary into one clear, trustworthy daily number so
people always know what they can safely spend before their next payday.

## The problem we are solving

Most personal finance apps show users what already happened. They provide
transactions, charts, budgets, and account balances, but still leave the user
to answer the most immediate question:

> Can I afford to spend this today and still make it safely to payday?

Salaried people often experience a repeating cycle:

1. Salary arrives and the account balance looks healthy.
2. Bills, investments, savings commitments, and daily expenses compete for the
   same money.
3. The remaining balance gives a false sense of what is actually available.
4. Spending decisions become guesswork as payday gets closer.
5. Anxiety increases, even when the person earns and plans responsibly.

Traditional calendar-month budgets also fail people whose financial life is
organized around salary dates. SalaryFlow treats payday as the center of the
money cycle.

## Our solution

SalaryFlow calculates a live **Safe to Spend Today** amount:

```text
Safe to spend per day =
  (Income - expenses - investments - planned savings) / days until next salary

Safe to spend today =
  safe to spend per day - amount already spent today
```

The app brings expenses, recurring bills, savings goals, investments, salary
history, and financial-year analytics into one system. Its purpose is not to
show more financial data. Its purpose is to turn that data into a clear daily
decision.

## Target audience

### Primary audience

Digitally comfortable salaried professionals in India who:

- receive a monthly or regular salary;
- manage recurring bills, subscriptions, investments, and savings goals;
- want control without maintaining complex spreadsheets;
- understand their bank balance but do not know what is genuinely disposable;
- want a calm daily answer rather than financial jargon.

### Initial user mindset

The user is not necessarily financially inexperienced. They are time-poor,
decision-fatigued, and unsure whether the money visible in their account is safe
to use.

### Future audience

The salary-cycle model can later support weekly and biweekly earners and users
outside India, while keeping the same core promise.

## Jobs to be done

When I am about to spend money, help me understand what is safe today so I do
not compromise bills, investments, savings, or the days remaining until payday.

When my salary arrives, help me assign it with intention and see the effect of
every commitment before I begin spending.

When I review past months or financial years, help me understand where my money
went and whether my financial position is improving.

## Value proposition

### Functional value

- One live daily spending allowance based on the salary cycle.
- Commitments are accounted for before disposable money is shown.
- Bills, goals, expenses, investments, and history stay connected.
- India financial-year views make historical reporting familiar and relevant.
- Mobile-first access and PWA support make the answer available at the moment
  of purchase.

### Emotional value

- **Clarity:** one answer instead of a dashboard full of interpretation.
- **Control:** spending becomes intentional rather than restrictive.
- **Calm:** fewer surprises near payday.
- **Confidence:** users can spend without second-guessing every decision.

## Positioning

For salaried people who are uncertain what they can afford between paydays,
SalaryFlow is a salary-cycle money companion that converts income and
commitments into a clear daily safe-to-spend amount. Unlike generic expense
trackers, SalaryFlow guides the next decision instead of only documenting the
last one.

## Brand promise

**Spend with clarity. Arrive at payday in control.**

## Brand personality

The identity should feel:

- calm, not cold;
- intelligent, not complicated;
- trustworthy, not institutional;
- encouraging, not judgmental;
- precise, not rigid;
- modern, not trendy;
- financially credible, but still human.

## Brand principles

1. **Clarity over complexity** — reduce information to the decision that matters.
2. **Guidance over judgment** — help users adjust without shame or alarmism.
3. **Rhythm over restriction** — money should be paced across the salary cycle.
4. **Trust through transparency** — calculations and commitments must be clear.
5. **Progress over perfection** — small daily decisions create financial stability.

## Competitive distinction

SalaryFlow should not present itself as another wallet, ledger, bank, or
investment platform. Its ownable territory is the intersection of:

- **salary** — a predictable inflow;
- **flow** — money paced through time;
- **safety** — commitments protected before discretionary spending;
- **today** — an immediate, usable decision.

The strongest brand idea is **a controlled flow from payday to payday**.

## Logo strategy

### What the logo should communicate

The mark should suggest that money is being paced through a cycle with a clear,
protected amount available today. It must work as a small mobile app icon first,
then as a wordmark, favicon, notification icon, and monochrome stamp.

### Recommended concept territories

Explore one territory deeply or combine no more than two:

1. **Paced flow** — a continuous path divided into measured daily intervals.
2. **Payday cycle** — two points in a cycle with controlled movement between them.
3. **Protected remainder** — a larger amount separating into commitments and a
   clear safe portion.
4. **S + F monogram** — a reduced monogram built from a flowing path, measured
   segments, or negative-space threshold.
5. **Daily pulse** — a restrained rhythm representing the changing safe amount,
   without resembling a trading chart.

### Useful visual geometry

- a line or path moving through measured points;
- an open cycle rather than a generic closed loop;
- a protected center or negative-space allowance;
- repeated intervals that suggest days;
- balanced forward motion;
- simple geometry that remains legible at 16–24 pixels.

### Avoid

- generic wallets, coins, rupee symbols, piggy banks, and bank buildings;
- upward stock-market arrows or trading-chart imagery;
- shields with checkmarks unless highly abstracted;
- water drops or waves that make “flow” literal;
- neon fintech gradients as the entire identity;
- aggressive red/green trading language;
- luxury-finance crests, mascots, or ornamental monograms;
- a logo that implies lending, banking, investing, or cryptocurrency.

## Verbal identity

### Voice

Use short, direct, reassuring language. Explain the consequence of a number,
not only the number itself. The product should sound like a clear-headed money
companion, never a lecturer.

### Message hierarchy

1. **Primary:** Know what you can safely spend today.
2. **Secondary:** Pace your money from payday to payday.
3. **Support:** Protect bills, investments, and savings before you spend.

### Tagline directions

- Spend with clarity.
- Your money, paced to payday.
- Know what is safe today.
- Make every payday last.
- A clearer way to payday.

The current recommended line is **Spend with clarity.** It is concise, flexible,
and communicates the emotional result without overexplaining the calculation.

## Product KPIs

These measure whether SalaryFlow is solving the user problem. Final targets
should be set after a baseline cohort is available.

| KPI | Definition | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Onboarding completion | Percentage of new users who add salary, payday, commitments, and reach the dashboard | The core answer requires a complete starting picture |
| Time to first safe-to-spend value | Median time from registration to seeing a valid daily amount | Measures how quickly the brand promise becomes real |
| Safe-to-spend view rate | Percentage of active users who view the daily amount on a given day or week | Indicates that the number is useful in daily decisions |
| Expense capture rate | Percentage of active days with expenses recorded or synced | The guidance remains accurate only when spending is current |
| Bill coverage | Percentage of active users with recurring commitments entered | Measures whether protected commitments are represented |
| Payday success rate | Percentage of salary cycles completed without the available amount dropping below zero | Direct indicator of improved financial pacing |
| End-of-cycle variance | Difference between projected and actual money remaining at payday | Tests the trustworthiness of the calculation |
| 4-week and 12-week retention | Percentage of users returning after one and three salary cycles | Measures durable value beyond initial curiosity |
| Weekly active usage | Users who check guidance or record activity at least once per week | Reflects ongoing product relevance |
| User confidence score | Response to “I know what I can safely spend before payday” | Measures the emotional outcome the product promises |

## Suggested initial KPI targets

Use these as directional launch goals, then revise them against real behavior:

- at least 70% onboarding completion;
- under 3 minutes median time to first safe-to-spend value;
- at least 60% of activated users checking guidance weekly;
- at least 50% four-week retention among activated users;
- at least 35% twelve-week retention among activated users;
- at least 70% of retained users entering recurring bills;
- a 20% improvement in user confidence after two salary cycles;
- a decreasing projected-versus-actual payday variance across three cycles.

## Brand KPIs

These measure whether the identity communicates the intended position:

| KPI | Method | Success signal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Distinctiveness | Show the mark among finance-app competitors without names | Users can identify it again after a delayed recall test |
| Concept comprehension | Ask what the symbol suggests before explaining it | Responses mention flow, pacing, cycle, clarity, or control |
| Category fit | Ask which type of product the identity belongs to | Reads as personal finance without looking like a bank or trading app |
| Trust | Rate the identity from untrustworthy to trustworthy | Strong trust without appearing corporate or severe |
| Small-size recognition | Test the app icon at 16, 24, 32, and 48 pixels | Core form remains distinct and balanced at every size |
| Monochrome resilience | Test in one color on light and dark backgrounds | Meaning and recognition do not depend on gradients |
| Wordmark legibility | Test at mobile-header and notification sizes | Name remains immediately readable |
| Emotional fit | Ask users to choose descriptive attributes | Clarity, calm, confidence, and modernity rank highest |

## Identity deliverables

The finished brand system should include:

- primary logo mark;
- horizontal and compact wordmarks;
- mobile app icon and favicon;
- monochrome, light, and dark variants;
- clear-space and minimum-size rules;
- construction logic and rationale;
- primary and supporting color palette with accessibility guidance;
- display and interface typography recommendations;
- icon and illustration direction;
- logo use in dashboard, login, notification, and PWA contexts;
- social avatar and simple launch graphic;
- examples of incorrect use.

## Creative test

Before approving a direction, ask:

> Could this identity belong only to SalaryFlow, and does it make the movement
> from payday uncertainty to daily clarity visible without relying on a generic
> finance symbol?

If the answer is no, the concept needs further reduction or a stronger link to
the product's salary-cycle logic.
