# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Salaried individuals who need a clear, mobile-friendly view of income, spending, bills, savings, investments, and liquid account balances across each salary cycle.

## Product Purpose

SalaryFlow helps users understand what they can safely spend today while preserving an accurate personal record of salary credits, obligations, goals, investments, and bank balances. Success means users can update real financial data quickly and trust the resulting summary.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, and practical. The product should feel trustworthy and focused without becoming clinical or visually noisy.

## Anti-references

Avoid trading-terminal density, decorative fintech gradients, marketing-style dashboard cards, hidden financial assumptions, and interactions that make consequential balance changes without clear confirmation.

## Design Principles

- Show current money separately from planned money movements.
- Make the salary cycle the organizing context for financial decisions.
- Prefer familiar controls and explicit statuses over clever interactions.
- Keep important totals scannable while preserving source-level detail.
- Never invent or silently infer completed financial transactions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and keyboard operation. Support reduced motion, visible focus states, clear non-color status labels, readable financial formatting, and responsive layouts for mobile and desktop use.
